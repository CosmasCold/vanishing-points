ACT V — CONVERGENCE

Player question: “What is the Archive actually doing?”

33 — MOUNT WEATHER EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER
The signal exists. Establishes the 4.5 Hz carrier/resonance, 18 Hz harmonic, synchronized resonance, and that the phenomenon is not simply a conventional local transmitter.

34 — CHEYENNE MOUNTAIN COMPLEX
The signal is synchronized. Establishes phase relationship, signal structure, auxiliary monitoring history, geological propagation, and reference-frame implications.

The system has temporal structure. Establishes synchronized 4.5 Hz, the 03:14 anomalous call, numbers-station transmission, child’s laughter as part of the transmission, winter-solstice relationship, countdown toward 2047, temporal reference, and the Observer as the missing fourth reference.

Causal geometry becomes observable. Requires Mount Weather, Cheyenne Mountain, Raven Rock, all three triangle relationships, and the Signal hypothesis. The Null Point is not a portal or boss arena. The Atlas ultimately represents causal proximity. Endgame reaches Solstice/2047 and the three endings.
