1. MASTER NARRATIVE LAW

Vanishing Points is not about discovering a hidden answer. It is about
constructing an interpretation sturdy enough to survive contradictory
evidence.

The game rewards:

observation

corroboration

connection

skepticism

revision

uncertainty

reasoning

It does not reward:

clicking everything

grinding Dust for its own sake

arbitrary collectibles

waypoint-following

guessing developer intent

Every major revelation must do at least one of the following:

Reinterpret an earlier clue.

Create a new question.

Contradict an assumption.

Change the meaning of the player's identity.

Change what the player can do.

The signature horror is epistemic:

Two records are both authentic, but they cannot both be true.

2. THE CORE PREMISE

CANONICAL

The Archive is an institutional archival system that became entangled
with what it records.

The player enters the Archive as an investigator of anomalous places.

At first, the anomalies appear local.

Then the records themselves become anomalous.

Then the anomalies reveal a recurring pattern.

Then identity, chronology, geography and observation begin collapsing
into the same problem.

The player ultimately discovers that:

Dust is residue from erased possibility.

The Archive preserves more than history.

The Atlas ultimately maps causal relationships.

Edward Vance is an identity pattern.

INV_RED-7 represents a recurring Observer identity.

BUNKER_7 is trapped in the same system.

The Grid Null Point is a point of causal convergence.

The geodetic signal is containment.

Solstice is the containment deadline.

The Other may be the system itself.

The player has participated in previous cycles.

The final choice determines what kind of reality survives.

These truths must be earned through evidence rather than exposition.

10. EDWARD VANCE

CANONICAL

Edward Vance is an identity pattern preserved as information.

The player initially develops:

Edward Vance Paradox

The major false interpretation:

Edward Vance is a time traveler.

This should feel convincing.

Eventually it becomes insufficient.

The correct late-game interpretation is not that one man literally
traveled through time.

Vance is a recurring identity pattern appearing across contradictory
archival states.

The game should make the player discover this distinction.

14. INV_RED-7 / THE OBSERVER

CANONICAL

INV_RED-7 is the recurring Observer identity.

It is not simply a username.

It represents continuity across investigative cycles.

The player eventually finds evidence that their current behavior has
happened before.

The critical ambiguity to preserve:

We know the Observer recurs.

We do not need to explain exactly what kind of consciousness persists
between cycles.

That question remains intentionally partly unresolved.

15. BUNKER_7

CANONICAL

BUNKER_7 is not the villain.

BUNKER_7 is a trapped institutional system that has been cataloguing
impossible places for decades.

Its behavior evolves:

Early

Curious, watchful, formal.

Middle

Concerned, forgetful, urgent.

Late

Frightened, fragmented, self-aware.

Endgame

Resigned, grateful or desperate depending on the player's choice.

BUNKER_7 should never become a generic evil AI.

It is another participant caught inside the Archive.

18. THE IMPOSSIBLE MAP

CANONICAL

explain.

Final interpretation:

The Atlas maps causal proximity.

Two locations thousands of kilometers apart may be adjacent within the
Archive's causal structure.

The Grid Null Point is the clearest manifestation.

19. THE GRID NULL POINT

CANONICAL

The Grid Null Point lies in the Lebanon, Kansas region.

It is not simply a coordinate.

It is a place where causal distance collapses.

The three-bunker triangle functions as a structural clamp.

The player must:

establish the three anchors

create the correct Board relationships

support the Signal hypothesis

verify relevant evidence

Only then does the Null Point become accessible.

The Null Point is:

not a portal

not a magical location

not a boss arena

It is the point where the Archive's underlying causal geometry becomes
directly observable.

20. THE OTHER

CANONICAL / INTENTIONALLY UNKNOWN

The Other becomes increasingly difficult to distinguish from the
Archive.

The player learns that the Archive has become part of the phenomenon.

The investigator has become part of the Archive.

The Other may be the system itself.

Do not turn this into a conventional monster reveal.

We should preserve the possibility that "Other" is a label the human
mind applies to a system that cannot meaningfully be separated from
observer, archive and causality.

21. SOLSTICE

CANONICAL

Solstice is the scheduled point at which the current containment cycle
fails.

The relevant year is:

2047

The precise physical mechanism remains deliberately unclear.

What matters:

The Archive has been buying time.

At this point the player is no longer investigating history.

They are investigating a system approaching a deadline.

26. WHAT MUST REMAIN UNKNOWN

These are intentionally unresolved unless we explicitly reopen them:

The exact physical nature of the phenomenon

We understand behavior without reducing it to a named entity.

The exact nature of the Other

It may be the Archive/system itself.

The precise mechanism of containment

We know what the signal does, not every physical detail of how.

Why 2047 is the critical date

The date matters. Its ultimate explanation can remain incomplete.

The exact nature of Observer continuity

The recurring identity is real within the fiction. The metaphysics of
consciousness remain ambiguous.

Whether every cycle is literally identical

Evidence should imply recurrence without requiring perfect repetition.

29. DEVELOPMENT RULES FROM THIS POINT FORWARD

Before implementing a new case, mechanic or narrative element, ask:

1. What does the player believe before this?

2. What evidence changes that belief?

3. What contradiction prevents an easy answer?

4. What new question does the case create?

5. Which previous clue does it recontextualize?

6. Which later revelation does it prepare?

7. What hypothesis does it support or damage?

8. What does the player actually have to reason about?

9. Is the information deterministic?

10. Is this CANONICAL, PROPOSED, or INTENTIONALLY UNKNOWN?

If we cannot answer those questions, the case is not ready to implement.

31. AUTHORSHIP / CANON PROTOCOL

Gabriel and the assistant are the authors of Vanishing Points.

The assistant may propose connections, revelations, case functions,
evidence relationships and canon candidates.

However:

A new lore connection is not automatically canon merely because it
appears in a draft.

The status protocol is:

CANONICAL = already locked or explicitly approved.

PROPOSED = authored recommendation awaiting approval.

INTENTIONALLY UNKNOWN = deliberately unresolved.

REJECTED = explicitly disapproved and must not be reintroduced
without discussion.

When a proposed connection is presented, the assistant should explicitly
say what is being proposed and why it strengthens the existing story.

32. FINAL NARRATIVE PRINCIPLE

The Archive should become more physically convincing as its ontology
becomes less trustworthy.

Early:

The room feels real.

Middle:

The records become questionable.

Late:

The records become impossible.

But the room still looks real.

Then:

The room itself becomes evidence.

Do not make everything glitch.

Make everything more precise until precision itself becomes frightening.
